## What was deferred and why
`sitemap.xml` is one file. It is capped at 50,000 URLs (the sitemaps.org per-file limit): the repository query bounds the result, and when more PUBLISHED products exist than fit, the list is truncated and `seo.sitemap_truncated` is logged. A sitemap index with several files (Next.js `generateSitemaps`) was not built because the catalog has no inventory and the cap is far above any realistic MVP size.

Two related choices are deliberate, not debt: the sitemap has no `lastmod` (the evidence tables do not bump `products.updatedAt`, and an inaccurate date is worse than none), and paginated category URLs are not enumerated (per the product-owner decision, only base category pages).

## Risk if left unresolved
Beyond 50,000 PUBLISHED products the tail would silently drop out of the sitemap (with a log line). Not a risk at MVP scale.

## Proposed resolution
When the catalog approaches the cap, split into a sitemap index using `generateSitemaps`, and add a reliable content-modified timestamp so `lastmod` can be emitted accurately. Add a test at the boundary.
