## What was deferred and why
`CompatibilityEvidenceStatus.CREATOR_DECLARED` and `SupportStatus.CREATOR_SUPPORTED`
(`packages/db/prisma/schema/evidence.prisma`) are real, shipped Postgres enum values
(MVP-005, vocabulary corrected by TD-008 on 2026-09-24). They are displayed verbatim
today as **"Creator Declared"** (`packages/domain/catalog/src/compatibility.ts:54`) and
**"Creator-supported"** (`packages/domain/catalog/src/support.ts:6`) on real, live
product pages. `schemas/product-metadata.schema.json`'s `support` enum also lists
`"creator"` as a literal allowed value.

The product owner's first-party-only publishing decision
(`docs/final-decisions.md`, "First-party-only publishing model", section 9) means
there is no third-party creator distinct from LowCodeStacks itself to attribute these
labels to. The decision records a follow-up presentation choice — **`CREATOR_DECLARED`
should display as "Publisher declared"** — without changing the underlying enum. It
explicitly does not resolve `CREATOR_SUPPORTED`'s replacement wording, since the exact
intended evidence meaning (who is making the support commitment, and what it means
under first-party-only — is it the same commitment as before, or does "first-party
supported" mean something subtly different from "creator-supported" did?) needs its
own review, not a mechanical find-and-replace.

**Deliberately not done in the same pass that recorded this decision**: no enum value
was renamed or removed (Postgres cannot drop an enum value without a table rebuild —
the same constraint TD-008's own migration already documented — and no destructive
change was approved regardless); no validator changed; no production presentation
code changed; no snapshot or test changed. The decision that authorized recording this
gap was documentation-and-planning-only.

## Risk if left unresolved
- A real, live product page shows "Creator Declared" and "Creator-supported" on a
  platform that, per its own now-decided business model, has no creators — a
  user-facing wording inaccuracy, not a functional defect.
- Every place these values appear (display labels, any documentation, any test
  assertion that pins the exact string) needs to be found and reviewed together, not
  patched one call site at a time, once the replacement wording is decided.

## Proposed resolution
Once `CREATOR_SUPPORTED`'s replacement wording is decided (a product-owner review, not
an engineering judgment call, per the authorizing decision's own instruction to
preserve the exact intended evidence meaning): update the display-label maps in
`packages/domain/catalog/src/compatibility.ts` and `support.ts`, and
`schemas/product-metadata.schema.json`'s corresponding documentation/enum-description
text, to the approved first-party wording. Search every user-facing presentation,
documentation reference, and test assertion where `CREATOR_DECLARED`, `Creator
Declared`, `CREATOR_SUPPORTED`, or `Creator-supported` appears before changing
anything, so no call site is missed. The underlying `CompatibilityEvidenceStatus` and
`SupportStatus` enum *values* are not renamed by this resolution — only their
*display* text.
