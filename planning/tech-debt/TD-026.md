## What was deferred and why
The approved "Guides hub by goal" structure board (`DL-Guides` on the design canvas; `docs/final-decisions.md`, "Structure boards approved") draws `/learn` with:
- a "What are you trying to do?" hero with a search box ("Paste an error, or describe the problem…");
- a row of goal chips (Fix a problem · Choose the right tool · Design it to last · Measure success) that jump to each section;
- a technology filter row (All technologies, the six technologies, Governance & admin);
- the four goal sections, in that order, each with its count and one line.

MVP-033 slice C shipped only part of the last item: the section headings and their order now match the board (`apps/web/lib/article-types.ts`), and the footer's Guides column uses the same goal labels. The line under each heading (`apps/web/app/learn/page.tsx`) is worded differently from the board's. The hero still reads "Every guide, in one place." with technology tiles, and there are no goal chips or technology filter. This kept slice C to the steps the handoff named and the PR small.

## Risk if left unresolved
The Guides page doesn't yet lead with the problem a visitor has, which is the point of organising it by goal. No content or links are lost: every guide is still listed and every technology is one click away.

## Proposed resolution
Rebuild the `/learn` hero to the board, and use the board's line under each section: the problem-first search (posting to the existing `/search`), the goal chips as in-page links to the section anchors (unchanged: `tutorials`, `comparisons`, `patterns`, `kpi-guides`), and the technology filter as links to each hub. Add the a11y gate state for it.

## Trigger to revisit
Before MVP-033 is marked Done.

## Resolution (2026-10-03)
Resolved in MVP-033 slice C (PR #62). `/learn` now matches the board:
- a "What are you trying to do?" hero with a problem-first search that posts to `/search`;
- goal chips linking to each section's anchor (shown only for sections that have guides);
- a technology filter (`/learn?technology=<slug>`) with All technologies, the six technologies and Governance & admin. An unknown value shows every guide. An area with no guides yet points to its hub. Filtered views keep `/learn` as their canonical URL;
- the board's line and coloured marker under each goal heading.

The technology tiles that linked to each hub were removed from `/learn`, as the board has none. Every hub is still linked from the header menu and the footer. Tests in `apps/web/lib/seo/pages.test.tsx`. axe is clean at 375 and 1280 px in both themes.
