## What was deferred and why
MVP-005 delivers the compatibility model, its display, and the write-time rules. The rules live in `validateCompatibilityEntry` (`packages/domain/catalog/src/compatibility.ts`): a platform area is required with a release wave, the release year is in range (2019 through current year + 2), the wave is 1 or 2, **Tested requires an evidence summary and a last-verified date**, verified dates cannot be in the future, notes and summaries are capped at 500 characters, and invisible/bidirectional-override characters are stripped. The structural rules are also enforced by database CHECK constraints (migration `20260921000000_add_product_evidence`).

But the story has no creator or moderator write surface — the creator editor (MVP-012) and moderation queue (MVP-013) own that — so:
1. **Nothing calls `validateCompatibilityEntry` yet.** Today evidence rows can only be created by direct database access.
2. **There is no automated screening of notes and evidence summaries for private data.** The product owner's 2026-09-21 decision says evidence text must never expose private test-environment details, credentials, tenant identifiers, customer information or internal operational data. `normalizeDisplayText` removes spoofing characters and React escapes markup, but nothing detects a GUID-shaped tenant ID or a secret-looking string. That kind of screening is heuristic and belongs with the write path and the moderator's review.
3. **Who may assign "Tested" is unresolved** (`docs/open-questions.md` item 6, part 2). The database enforces "Tested requires evidence" but not "Tested was reviewed by staff".

## Risk if left unresolved
Once a write path exists, a creator could self-assign "Tested", or paste a tenant identifier into a public note, if that path skips the validator and reviewers aren't checking. Today the exposure is nil (no write path), which is why this is recorded rather than built now.

## Proposed resolution
- MVP-012 must call `validateCompatibilityEntry` on every save and surface its error codes to the creator.
- MVP-013's review checklist must include an evidence review step.
- Resolve open question 6, part 2 before MVP-012 lets a creator submit `TESTED`; until then default creator submissions to `CREATOR_DECLARED`.
- Add a heuristic private-data check (GUID/tenant-ID and secret-shaped strings) as a warning to the reviewer, not as the sole control.
