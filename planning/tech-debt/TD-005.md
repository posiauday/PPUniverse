## What was deferred and why
FR-002 lists filters for "Power Apps, Power Automate, Power BI, Architecture, Governance, AI, asset type, free/paid, license, compatibility, accessibility status, and update recency". MVP-004 delivered keyword search, category (asset type) filter, sort and pagination — everything the schema supported at the time. The remaining axes were deliberately not built because the fields they filter on did not exist:
- **license** and **compatibility** — fields owned by MVP-005 (schema and display). MVP-005 exposes the structured `platformArea` / `minReleaseYear` / `minReleaseWave` and license-tier data so a filter can be built, but by product-owner instruction builds no filtering UI itself.
- **free/paid** — needs price/pricing-model data owned by MVP-007 (commerce).
- **accessibility status** — no story currently owns an accessibility-status field (FR-003 lists an accessibility statement on the product page; MVP-005's acceptance criteria do not include it).
- **update recency** — sorting by newest exists; a recency *filter* was not built.
- **"AI"** — listed in FR-002 but not one of the six locked asset categories (`docs/final-decisions.md` 2026-09-17); whether it is a category, a filter, or a reference to something like Copilot Studio is unresolved (`docs/open-questions.md` item 25). Not resolved here.

MVP-004 was recorded as "Implemented" for FR-002 in `planning/requirement-traceability.csv`; that overstated the coverage and has been corrected to "Partially Implemented" (found while starting MVP-005, per `CLAUDE.md`: a gap found in delivered work is logged rather than left unrecorded).

## Risk if left unresolved
Users can search, sort, and filter by category but not by license or compatibility, which are central to the "trust" positioning (`docs/05-ux-design-system.md`: evidence before promotional copy). Low security risk; a product-completeness gap against FR-002.

## Proposed resolution
Needs a product-owner backlog decision: extend MVP-004 (explicitly approved, per the 2026-09-21 compatibility decision) or add a follow-up story for license and compatibility filters once MVP-005 lands; free/paid follows MVP-007; accessibility-status needs an owning story and field first; "AI" needs clarification (open question 25).
