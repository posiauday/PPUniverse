## What was deferred and why
The technology hubs (MVP-033 slice C; `docs/final-decisions.md`, "Technology pages are hubs") group each area's guides into its own researched sections. To ship the hubs before the content-model change (slice B):
- Each area's sections live in `HUB_TOPICS` in `apps/web/lib/technology-hubs.ts`.
- Each launch guide's section comes from `ARTICLE_TOPIC`, a slug-to-section map in the same file. A guide not in the map is shown in its area's first section, so a published guide is never hidden.
- The Governance & admin hub (`/governance`) shows only sections and planned guides. Governance is not yet a `Technology` value, so no guide can be tagged to it.

## Risk if left unresolved
- A new guide lands in the wrong section until a developer adds its slug to the map. Editors can't choose a guide's section in the admin.
- Renaming a guide's slug silently moves it to the first section.
- Governance guides can't be published to their own hub.

## Proposed resolution
Slice B of `docs/plans/mvp-033-navigation-restructure.md`:
- Add a nullable `topic` column to articles and an `GOVERNANCE_ADMIN` area value, in a reversible migration.
- Add a topic picker in the admin editor, limited to the chosen area's sections.
- Backfill from `ARTICLE_TOPIC`, then delete the map.

## Trigger to revisit
Before the first guide published after launch, or the first Governance & admin guide, whichever comes first.

## Resolution (2026-10-03)
Resolved by MVP-033 slice B:
- **Storage:** a nullable `articles.topic` column (migration `20261003000100_add_article_topic`). The migration backfills the 24 launch guides, matching on slug and technology and never overwriting a topic that is already set.
- **Governance:** `GOVERNANCE_ADMIN` becomes a `Technology` value (migration `20261003000000_add_governance_admin_technology`).
- **Validation:** `TECHNOLOGY_TOPICS` and `isValidTopic` in `@ppu/domain-content`, used by the admin API and the content importer. All 24 launch files carry `topic:`.
- **Admin editor:** a "Hub section" picker that depends on the technology.
- **Hubs:** they group by the stored topic, and `ARTICLE_TOPIC` is deleted.
- **Governance & admin page:** it lists its published guides, becomes indexable once it has one, and joins the sitemap.
