## What was deferred and why
`apps/web/app/learn/page.tsx` lists up to 500 published articles (`LEARN_INDEX_LIMIT`), newest first, grouped by type. Pagination was left out to land the hub: at launch the library will hold tens of articles, not hundreds.

## Risk if left unresolved
Once more than 500 articles are published, the oldest ones drop off the hub. They stay in the sitemap and are still linked from related-article lists, so crawlers can find them, but the hub stops being a complete index and the page grows heavy long before that point.

## Proposed resolution
Paginate the hub (`/learn?page=N`) with the same canonical and indexing rules as category pages (`lib/seo/category-indexing.ts`). Alternatively, split it by type into `/learn/tutorials` and similar hubs, which fits the per-technology structure the product owner has asked for.

## Trigger to revisit
The library reaches 300 published articles, or per-technology hubs are built, whichever comes first.
