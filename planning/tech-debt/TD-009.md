## What was deferred and why
MVP-021 makes the indexable pages (home, category, product) indexable whenever they are served with a valid `NEXT_PUBLIC_SITE_URL`. Nothing distinguishes a **production** deployment from a **staging or preview** deployment that is also served over a public `https` URL: both would emit `index, follow`, a canonical, and a sitemap. There is no environment switch that forces `noindex` (and an empty sitemap) for non-production environments.

It was deferred because the hosting model — and therefore what preview and staging environments exist and how they are addressed — is undecided (`docs/open-questions.md` item 5), and inventing a switch now would guess at that model.

## Risk if left unresolved
A publicly reachable staging or preview deployment could be crawled and indexed, competing with production and exposing unpublished-looking test content. Low today (no deployment exists); it becomes real as soon as a second public environment does.

## Proposed resolution
When the hosting decision is made, add an explicit environment setting (for example a "site environment" value, default production-safe) that forces `noindex, nofollow` on every page, disables the sitemap and structured data, and serves a `Disallow: /` robots.txt for non-production. Add tests for each mode. Decide whether it also reads a platform-provided variable.
