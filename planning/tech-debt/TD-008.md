## What was deferred and why
The product owner's statuses decision (`docs/final-decisions.md` 2026-09-21, section B; open question 6 part 2) arrived after MVP-005 was merged. It says the only statuses assignable for current publication are **Creator Declared** and **Marketplace Reviewed**, and that **Tested is reserved** (not assignable, never inferred, never migrated to, no user-facing badge or filter, not removed destructively). MVP-005 implements the earlier three-state vocabulary. This was not reconciled in the same turn because the instruction limits MVP-021 to FR-017 and says not to modify MVP-005's completed status.

Mismatches, all in merged code:
1. **Database enum** `CompatibilityEvidenceStatus` is `TESTED`, `CREATOR_DECLARED`, `NOT_VERIFIED` and has no `MARKETPLACE_REVIEWED`. Adding a value is additive (`ALTER TYPE ... ADD VALUE`); `TESTED` must not be removed.
2. **Validator** `validateCompatibilityEntry` accepts `TESTED` as an ordinary assignable status; nothing marks it reserved.
3. **On-page legend** (`EVIDENCE_LEGEND`) defines Tested, Creator Declared and Not Verified, so it would display "Tested" as soon as any compatibility row exists. The decision says not to display Tested unless valid historical data exists with documented provenance. The Creator Declared definition also differs from the decision's wording.
4. **Labels and badge** — `MARKETPLACE_REVIEWED` has no label; `TESTED` is rendered like any other status.
5. **Tests** assert the old vocabulary: `compatibility.test.ts`, `present-evidence.test.ts`, `product-evidence.test.tsx`, and the constraint tests in `catalog-repository.integration.test.ts` (which use `TESTED` to prove the "Tested requires evidence" CHECK — that CHECK remains correct for the reserved value and needs no change).
6. **Docs** — `packages/domain/catalog/README.md` and the MVP-005 progress-report entry describe the old vocabulary.

No user-visible harm exists today: there are no product or compatibility rows and nothing can write them (TD-006).

## Risk if left unresolved
Once any compatibility row exists the legend tells buyers what "Tested" means although no product can be Tested, and a moderator-assigned Marketplace Reviewed cannot be stored. Once MVP-012/013 write paths exist, creators could be offered a status the decision prohibits.

## Proposed resolution
A small corrective change, **after** the product owner answers open question 28 and **before** MVP-012/013 allow anything to be written:
1. Migration adds `MARKETPLACE_REVIEWED` (and settles `NOT_VERIFIED`); never removes `TESTED`.
2. Validator treats `TESTED` as reserved; accepts `CREATOR_DECLARED` and `MARKETPLACE_REVIEWED`. Role rules — creators cannot assign Marketplace Reviewed, moderators cannot assign Tested — are enforced at the write paths (MVP-012/013).
3. Legend and labels show Creator Declared and Marketplace Reviewed with the decision's definitions; Tested renders only for documented historical data.
4. Update the tests and docs listed above.
