## What was deferred and why
The product owner's 2026-09-21 decision for MVP-023 creates branch protection on `develop` only, with a required pull request, zero required approvals, two required status checks, force-push and deletion blocked, and administrators not enforced. It deliberately leaves `main` unprotected and sets no reviewer or approval rules. Protection of `main`, reviewer and approval rules, any ruleset strategy and repository visibility remain the open remainder of `docs/open-questions.md` item 17.

## Risk if left unresolved
`main` can be pushed to, force-pushed or deleted without a pull request or passing checks, and no change on either branch needs a second person's review; administrators can override the `develop` rule. Delivery discipline ("never work on `main`", pull-request-only delivery, merge only after green CI) is currently procedural, not enforced by GitHub, for `main` and for review.

## Proposed resolution
When the product owner decides the rest of open question 17: protect `main` (pull request, required checks, no force-push or deletion), decide reviewer and approval rules and whether administrators are enforced, and decide the ruleset strategy. Record the decision in `docs/final-decisions.md` and apply it through a reviewed change, reporting the exact settings in the pull request.
