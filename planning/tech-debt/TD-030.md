## What was deferred and why
CI cancels each accessibility shard at `timeout-minutes: 20` (`.github/workflows/ci.yml`). On 2026-10-06 it cancelled a shard on four PRs, each with **no failing test**, so the PR was blocked until the failed jobs were re-run:
- **#76 and #77:** a shard cancelled; both passed on re-run.
- **#78:** shard 4 had passed all 477 of its tests, then was cancelled.
- **#81:** shard 4 had passed 368 tests with none failed, then was cancelled; re-run.

The shards are uneven: shard 4 is mostly WebKit, which runs slowest. The suite also grows with each story. Changing the time budget or the shard count is governed by `docs/final-decisions.md`, "Accessibility suite mitigation" (a standing stop-and-present trigger), so it isn't changed here without the product owner.

## Risk if left unresolved
- PRs look red for a reason that isn't a defect.
- Each one needs a re-run, so merges wait longer.
- A real failure could be mistaken for "just the timeout". Always check the log for failed tests before re-running.

## Proposed resolution
Pick one option and record it as a decision:
1. **Five shards instead of four,** or rebalance them so WebKit's states are spread out (no budget change).
2. **Raise the job limit,** for example to 30 minutes. This changes the CI budget, so it needs the product owner.
3. **Trim the dark-theme pass** to the widths that differ.

Option 1 keeps every check, so it's the recommended one.

## Trigger to revisit
The next time a shard is cancelled at the limit, or before the next story adds page states, whichever comes first.
