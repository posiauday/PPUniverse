## What was deferred and why
FR-003 (`docs/02-prd.md`) says product pages show "title, summary, creator, screenshots, demo, price, license, compatibility, prerequisites, setup, accessibility statement, support, changelog, version history and related assets". MVP-005's acceptance criterion is narrower — "All required license/version/support/compatibility fields display" — and that is what was delivered. Together with MVP-003 (title, summary, category) that leaves these FR-003 items with no delivering story:
- creator (and any creator-profile link; `/creators/[handle]` is already open question 24)
- screenshots and demo (media)
- price (pricing model is still open — open question 7)
- prerequisites and setup instructions
- accessibility statement
- changelog and full **version history** (MVP-005 shows only the current published version; the `Release` table is deliberately minimal — release immutability, files and changelog are MVP-012/014 scope)
- related assets

Also deferred: a **Playwright E2E journey** for the product detail page. `planning/requirement-traceability.csv` had planned "E2E product detail" as FR-003's test evidence, but E2E tooling is not configured anywhere in the repo yet (`CLAUDE.md`: added by the first story that needs a full browser journey). Coverage for this story is unit tests, DB-gated integration tests (including the database constraints), a manual browser check at desktop and mobile widths, and an axe-core scan.

FR-003 traceability is therefore recorded as "Partially Implemented", not "Implemented" (the same correction made for FR-002 in TD-005).

## Risk if left unresolved
The product page can show evidence (license, version, support, compatibility) but not the surrounding content a buyer expects, and no backlog story is guaranteed to add it. Low security risk; a product-completeness and trust-positioning gap against FR-003.

## Proposed resolution
**Dispositions recorded 2026-09-21** (`docs/final-decisions.md` section C; open question 26): creator → MVP-011 (ownership only); price → MVP-007; screenshots and demo → proposed PROP-001; prerequisites and setup → proposed PROP-002; accessibility statement → proposed PROP-003; changelog and version history → proposed PROP-004; related assets → deferred (PROP-005). The proposals are in `planning/proposed-stories.md` and are **not approved**. Fold the product-detail E2E journey into whichever story first introduces Playwright (likely MVP-023 or MVP-025).
