## What was done and why
On 2026-10-03 the CI dependency audit (`pnpm audit --audit-level=high`) started failing on every branch. The cause is GHSA-vfj7-8cjw-p6xm: `braces` <=3.0.3 can exhaust the stack on deeply nested patterns, and there is no patched release.

`braces` reached us through a single path: the lint-time dev dependency `@next/eslint-plugin-next` (latest 16.3.8 still pins `fast-glob` 3.3.1), then `fast-glob`, then `micromatch`, then `braces`. A `pnpm-workspace.yaml` override now gives the plugin `tinyglobby` in place of `fast-glob`:

```
"@next/eslint-plugin-next>fast-glob": "npm:tinyglobby@^0.2.17"
```

`braces` is then no longer installed (`pnpm why braces` is empty), and the audit is clean. Nothing was ignored or hidden.

## Why it is safe
- **One call site:** the plugin calls one fast-glob function, `globSync(pattern, { onlyDirectories: true })`, in `dist/utils/get-root-dirs.js`.
- **Same signature:** `tinyglobby` exports the same `globSync` with that option, and uses `picomatch`, not `braces`.
- **Not used in our config:** the call runs only when `settings.next.rootDir` is set, which our `eslint.config.js` doesn't do.
- **Checked by hand:** `getRootDirs` returns the cwd with no setting, and the matching directories for `rootDir: "apps/*"`.
- **Lint unchanged:** it passes in every package.

## Risk if left unresolved
A future plugin version could call more of fast-glob's API, which tinyglobby may not provide. Lint would then fail loudly, not silently.

## Proposed resolution
Remove the override once `@next/eslint-plugin-next` stops depending on `fast-glob` 3 (or `braces` ships a fix), and re-run `pnpm audit`.

## Trigger to revisit
Every upgrade of `@next/eslint-plugin-next`: check `pnpm why braces` and whether the override is still needed.
