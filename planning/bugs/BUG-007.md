Follows `planning/github/04-bug-template.md`. Found while measuring the accessibility baseline for MVP-023.

## Summary
`ProductCard` renders its title through `CardTitle`, which is an `h3`. On `/categories/[slug]` and `/search` the page has an `h1` and then the cards' `h3` titles with no `h2` between, so the heading outline skips a level.

## Severity
- [x] P3 — minor content or admin issue

## Affected requirement / feature
- NFR-001. axe `heading-order` (best-practice; relates to WCAG 1.3.1 Info and Relationships as an advisory technique, not a definite A/AA failure). `docs/05` requires a logical heading structure.
- Delivered by MVP-003/004 (`packages/ui/src/card.tsx`, `product-card.tsx`; the category and search pages).

## Environment
Local dev server, axe-core 4.10.2, 4 widths, 2026-09-21. Guest.

## Steps to reproduce
Open `/categories/power-apps-components`, `/categories/power-apps-components?page=2`, `/search` or `/search?q=Verify` and read the heading outline (h1, then h3 ×12).

## Expected behavior
No skipped heading level (for example an `h2` section heading such as "Products", or a card title level that follows the page structure).

## Actual behavior
axe reports `heading-order` (moderate) on all four pages at every width tested. The home page and product pages do not trigger it.

## Evidence
44 axe scans (11 pages × 4 widths): the finding is identical at 1280, 768, 375 and 320 px.

## Security or privacy impact
- [ ] None.

## Accessibility impact
- [x] Affects heading navigation for screen-reader users. Apply `type:accessibility`.

## Suggested fix
Add a visually appropriate `h2` for the results list, or make the card title level a prop. Assert the outline in the MVP-023 pages spec.

## Resolution
Fixed by MVP-023 F4 (commit bfc7246): a visually hidden `h2` before the results list on `/search` and category pages. Regression test: `bug-007-heading-order.spec.ts`, shown failing before the fix. The advisory axe `heading-order` finding no longer appears on any real page in the CI run summary.
