Follows `planning/github/04-bug-template.md`. Found in the MVP-023 accessibility CI job.
Decision: `docs/final-decisions.md`, "Product-owner decision — BUG-013 and signin-sent"
(2026-09-21). Deliberately kept separate from BUG-013 — a different page, a different
mechanism, and (so far) a single, non-recurring observation.

## Summary
In CI run 4 (`e43e1aa`), the `signin-sent @ 320px` state (`tests/a11y/pages.spec.ts`)
failed in Firefox: after intercepting the auth API to simulate a successful send and
submitting the form, the `role="status"` region never showed "check your email" within
the 7.5s timeout. It has not recurred in runs 1, 2, 3 or 5 (5 runs total; failed once).

## Severity
- [x] P3 — minor content or admin issue (a single non-recurring observation; does not
  block the story per the product owner's explicit decision)

## Affected requirement / feature
- NFR-001. Delivered by MVP-002 (`apps/web/app/signin/page.tsx`), exercised by MVP-023
  F6/F8.

## Environment
CI (`ubuntu-latest`), production build, Firefox 155.0 via Playwright 1.63.0, one
occurrence out of five runs so far.

## Steps to reproduce
Not reliably reproducible. Observed once: navigate to `/signin`, intercept
`POST /api/auth/signin/email` to simulate success, submit a valid email, wait for the
status region to say "check your email".

## Actual behavior (from the one occurrence, run 4, before failure-evidence
instrumentation existed)
The Playwright trace showed: the click completed, `waiting for scheduled navigations to
finish` resolved immediately, and the status locator resolved repeatedly to an empty
`<p role="status">`. No `/api/auth/*` request appears in the action trace after the
click, and no console or page errors were recorded (that run predates the dedicated
console/network capture added for BUG-013; only Playwright's own action trace was
available). Whether the click failed to reach the handler, the interception did not
apply in time, or something else is unresolved.

## Decision (2026-09-21, direct product-owner instruction)
- **Monitor, do not fix.** No fix is proposed or applied without fresh evidence.
- **No workaround either**: no retry, no wait, no timeout change, and no conditional
  skip for this spec.
- **Does not block MVP-023.** It is not reproducing, the failure-evidence
  instrumentation (`packages/e2e/src/failure-evidence*.ts`, added for BUG-013) is
  permanently in place and costs nothing on passing runs, and holding a completed story
  open against one non-recurring observation would be indefinite.
- **This record stays open past merge** and is the first place to check if it recurs.

## If it recurs
Classify it against the fresh console/network/title evidence the instrumentation now
captures automatically on any failure, and stop for approval before proposing or
applying anything — same process as BUG-013.
