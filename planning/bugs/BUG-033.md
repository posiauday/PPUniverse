## Summary
At 375 px, the Text field's live preview (340 px wide) sat in a preview screen with 285 px of room, and its right edge was cut off: the end of the text box and the character count didn't show. Found while checking the new People picker at phone width, where "Add me" was cut off the same way. The page didn't scroll sideways, so the accessibility gate's overflow check didn't see it.

## Cause
The preview screen centres the component in a CSS grid with an automatic column. An automatic column grows to fit its content, so `max-w-full` on the component meant "as wide as the column" (340 px), not "as wide as the screen". The screen's `overflow-hidden` then hid the rest.

## Fix
Both grids in the workbench's preview screen now use a `minmax(0, 1fr)` column, so the column is the screen's width and each component shrinks to fit (`apps/web/app/components/ComponentWorkbench.tsx`).

Checked locally on a production build: all nine component previews fit inside their screen at 320 and 375 px. The People picker passes axe in 40 states (light and dark, 375 and 1280 px).

## Expected behavior
A component preview is never wider than its screen. On a phone, it narrows to fit, as it would on a narrow Power Apps screen.

## Accessibility impact
- [x] Fixed: WCAG 1.4.10 Reflow. At 320 px, nothing in the preview is hidden.
