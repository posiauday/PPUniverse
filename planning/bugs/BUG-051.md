Follows `planning/github/04-bug-template.md`. Found in the admin centre review the product owner asked for on 2026-10-10.

## Summary
On /admin/settings the Component library switch's knob slid past the end of its track and covered the word On, which read as "Dn".

## Cause
A button centres its content, so the knob (absolutely positioned with no left) started in the middle of the track and its 22 px slide pushed it off the end.

## Fix
The knob is pinned to the track's left edge (left-0), so On and Off both sit inside the track.
