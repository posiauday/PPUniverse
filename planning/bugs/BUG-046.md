Follows `planning/github/04-bug-template.md`. Found in the full-site code review the product owner asked for on 2026-10-10.

## Summary
Google sign-in attaches to an existing account with the same email (allowDangerousEmailAccountLinking). Nothing checked Google's email_verified claim, which Auth.js requires for that setting to be safe.

## Cause
MVP-035 relied on Google verifying emails, without checking the claim that says so.

## Fix
A signIn callback (googleEmailVerified in lib/google-auth.ts) refuses a Google sign-in unless email_verified is exactly true. Other sign-in methods are unaffected.

## Steps to reproduce
Sign in with a Google account whose email is someone else's and unverified.

## Expected behavior
Access denied.

## Actual behavior
Signed in as the owner of that email, including an admin.

## Security or privacy impact
Possible account takeover. No sign of it having happened.

## Tests
google-auth.test.ts (verified, unverified, missing, the string "true", other providers).
