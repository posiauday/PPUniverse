## Summary
Found by the product owner while scrolling a guide (with screenshots). On wide screens, the "On this page" list was sticky, but the **Copy link** button under it was not part of the sticky block. While scrolling, the button slid up underneath the list and they overlapped.

The same pass found two more problems in the new side column (the guide frame, #86):
- it was sticky but taller than the window, so its last cards couldn't be reached;
- a long word ("USERPRINCIPALNAME") overflowed a card.

## Severity
- [x] P3: a layout defect that makes reading harder. No data or security impact.

## Steps to reproduce
1. Open a long guide at 1024 px or wider, for example `/learn/security-roles-business-units-teams`.
2. Scroll down: Copy link moves up over the contents list.
3. At 1280 px or wider, the related-guides column cuts off below the window.

## Fix
- `StickyColumn` (`apps/web/app/learn/ArticleToc.tsx`) makes the whole left column sticky: the list and Copy link move together. It scrolls on its own when taller than the window.
- `ArticleToc` itself is no longer sticky. The guide page and the info pages (About, Privacy, Terms, How we write) both use the column.
- The side column scrolls on its own too, and lets long words wrap.
- **Checked by measuring:** at 0, 1,400 and 3,000 px of scroll, the list and the button never overlap. The side column scrolls, no card overflows, and the page has no sideways scroll.

## Accessibility impact
- [ ] Doesn't block keyboard or screen readers, but overlapping controls hurt use on screen (WCAG 1.4.10 and 2.4.11 spirit).
