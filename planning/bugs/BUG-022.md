## Summary
The admin article form (`apps/web/app/admin/content/ArticleForm.tsx`) showed a "Technology section" picker but left its value out of the request body. Both admin content APIs treat a missing technology as "none". So every create or save from the editor stored `technology = null`, and the article dropped out of its technology hub, the hub's sitemap entry and the header menu's counts. It still appeared on `/learn`.

Found while adding the MVP-033 hub-section (topic) picker to the same form.

## Severity
- [x] P2: a content-placement defect, with no security or data-loss impact beyond the one field.
- **Exposure:** any article saved through the editor in production since MVP-028 lost its section. The 24 launch guides were imported with their technology (`content:import`), and publishing (`ArticlePublishControl`) doesn't use the form, so they're affected only if someone edited and saved them. **Product owner check:** in the admin list, look for a guide that should have a technology but shows none, and re-save it with this fix.

## Steps to reproduce
1. As an admin, open `/admin/content/<id>/edit` for an article with a technology (for example Power BI).
2. Change the title and save.
3. The article's technology is now empty, and it no longer appears on `/power-bi`.

## Expected / actual
- **Expected:** the saved article keeps the technology shown in the picker.
- **Actual:** the technology was cleared on every save.

## Why it was not caught earlier
The API tests (MVP-028) checked that the API stores whatever technology it receives. Nothing tested that the form sends it, and the form has no component test.

## Resolution (2026-10-03)
- **The form now sends** `technology` and the new `topic` (MVP-033).
- **API tests** for both routes cover technology and topic round-trips and rejection.
- **No data migration:** an article cleared in production is restored by re-saving it with the right technology.
