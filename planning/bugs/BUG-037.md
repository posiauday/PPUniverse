## Summary
The product owner reported that "On this page" "does not work or move with scrolling on lots of pages". The panel itself does stick: measured on the live site at 1,080 and 1,280 px, it stays pinned while scrolling. But the highlight of the section being read lagged. Scrolling with the mouse wheel to each section on all 64 live pages (60 guides, About, Privacy, Terms, How we write) in Chromium and Firefox, every page marked the previous section, and the first section was never marked. So the list looked frozen.

## Cause
`TocSpy` counted a heading as "being read" only once it was within 140 px of the top of the window. The sticky header is about 100 px tall, so a section was marked only when its heading was almost under the header. While you read a section with its heading lower on the screen, where you'd naturally look, the list still marked the one before.

## Fix
The reading line is about a third of the way down the window, and never less than 160 px (`readingLine` in `apps/web/app/guides/TocSpy.tsx`). Unit tests cover the line and the marking.

Checked locally on a production build with the same 60 guides, scrolling to each section: in Chromium and Firefox the section scrolled to is now marked. The exception is a heading followed straight away by a sub-heading, where the sub-heading is marked; that's the more precise section, and correct. (Before the fix: 0 of 64 pages right in Chromium.)

## Expected behavior
As you scroll, "On this page" marks the section you're reading.

## Accessibility impact
- [x] Fixed: the marked link (`aria-current="location"`) now matches what's on screen, for sighted readers and screen reader users alike.
