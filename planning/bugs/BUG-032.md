## Summary
Reported by the product owner with a screenshot: signed in as an admin, the header's right side (search, Admin, the avatar with "Account", the theme toggle, Learn) dropped to a second row under the main links.

## Cause
The one-row bar is 1,217 pixels wide at most. Signed in as an admin, with the Updates "new" badge and (once switched on) Components, its parts needed about 1,225 pixels at 1280 wide, and far more between 1024 and 1279. Admin and the "Account" label were the newest additions (#112).

## Fix
- The header has no Admin link; the avatar alone is the Account button (screen readers hear "Account"). Admins reach Admin from the account menu (docs/final-decisions.md, 2026-10-08).
- Between 1024 and 1279 pixels the wordmark is for screen readers only; the logo stays.
- The nav links and the bar's gaps are a little tighter, and the search box is 16rem with its approved words in a slightly smaller size.
- Checked locally: one row at 1024, 1100, 1180, 1280, 1366 and 1440 pixels, signed in and signed out, with Components and the Updates badge showing.

## Expected behavior
The header is one row at every desktop width, whoever is signed in.

## Accessibility impact
- [x] Keeps WCAG 1.4.10 and 2.4.11 as before: the bar wraps only on phones, where it isn't sticky.
