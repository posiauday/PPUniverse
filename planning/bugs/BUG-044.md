Follows `planning/github/04-bug-template.md`. Found in the full-site code review the product owner asked for on 2026-10-10.

## Summary
/account/welcome sends a reader on to its callbackUrl after the Terms. Its check (safeContinuePath) refused "//host" and "/\\host" but not a slash, a tab or newline and a slash: "/%09/evil.example" passed, and the browser drops the tab and goes to "//evil.example". A repeated callbackUrl also made the page fail (500), because the check called startsWith on an array.

## Cause
Two separate redirect checks existed. The sign-in page's continuePath was hardened against this exact trick in BUG-040; the welcome page's copy was not.

## Fix
One shared rule, `sameSitePath` (apps/web/lib/same-site-path.ts): the value is resolved the way a browser would and kept only if it stays on the site. Both continuePath and safeContinuePath use it; anything that isn't a string falls back to /account.

## Steps to reproduce
Signed in, with the Terms accepted, open /account/welcome?callbackUrl=/%09/evil.example.

## Expected behavior
The reader lands on /account.

## Actual behavior
The reader landed on evil.example.

## Security or privacy impact
Phishing aid: a link on lowcodestacks.com that lands on any site.

## Tests
terms-acceptance.test.ts (tab, newline, backslashes, a repeated parameter); password-client.test.ts unchanged and passing.
