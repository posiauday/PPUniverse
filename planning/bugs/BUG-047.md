Follows `planning/github/04-bug-template.md`. Found in the full-site code review the product owner asked for on 2026-10-10.

## Summary
profileFromGoogle passed Google's email through as given, while the email link and passwords lowercase it, and the users' email index is case-sensitive.

## Cause
No normalisation in the Google profile mapping.

## Fix
The Google email is trimmed and lowercased like the others.

## Steps to reproduce
Sign up by email link as avery@contoso.com, then sign in with a Google profile reporting Avery@Contoso.com.

## Expected behavior
The same account.

## Actual behavior
A second account for the same person.

## Security or privacy impact
None on security; split profiles and comments.

google-auth.test.ts.
