# Business Requirements Document

## 1. Vision
Create the most useful independent destination for Power Platform practitioners to discover credible reusable assets, learn implementation patterns, evaluate compatibility, purchase licensed products, and connect with creators.

## 2. Business problem
The ecosystem is fragmented across official documentation, community forums, creator websites, videos, GitHub repositories, and generic digital-product stores. Buyers struggle to assess quality, compatibility, licensing, accessibility, maintenance, and enterprise suitability. Creators lack a specialized storefront with structured technical metadata and discovery.

## 3. Objectives
- Establish trust through transparent compatibility, licensing, versioning, documentation, moderation, and creator identity.
- Acquire users through high-intent free resources and indexable technical pages.
- Convert qualified users through premium components, templates, bundles, and subscriptions.
- Create repeat usage through saved assets, updates, learning paths, and changelogs.
- Add enterprise revenue through team licensing, governance packs, architecture libraries, and support.

## 4. Stakeholders
Product owner; end users; creators/sellers; marketplace moderators; customer support; finance; security/privacy; legal; content editors; engineering; analytics; enterprise buyers.

## 5. User segments
Power Apps developers; Power Automate developers; Power BI developers; solution architects; citizen developers; consultants; public-sector and enterprise teams; beginners; advanced practitioners.

## 6. MVP scope
### In scope
Public catalog, taxonomy, search/filter, asset detail pages, documentation, previews, accounts, free downloads, paid checkout, entitlements, creator onboarding, asset submission, moderation, version releases, reviews limited to verified purchasers/downloaders, admin operations, transactional email, analytics consent, SEO metadata, audit logs.

### Out of scope
Open community forum, live courses, certifications, hackathons, multi-vendor payout automation, tenant-wide scanners, AI solution architect, native mobile apps, direct deployment into customer tenants, escrow, consulting marketplace, and unsupported claims of Microsoft partnership.

## 7. Business rules
- A product cannot be published without owner, category, summary, documentation, support status, license, version, compatibility, price classification, media, and moderation decision.
- Paid access is granted only from a verified payment event or approved admin grant.
- Download authorization is checked at request time and logged.
- Historical purchases retain access to entitled versions according to the purchased license.
- Reviews require a verified qualifying interaction and are subject to moderation.
- Creator and buyer data are separated by authorization policy.
- Delete, suspend, refund, and takedown decisions create immutable audit events.

## 8. Success measures
Track acquisition, activation, conversion, retention, supply, quality, and reliability. Initial targets must be set only after baseline collection. Required metrics include organic landing sessions, search-to-detail rate, detail-to-download rate, checkout conversion, repeat download rate, creator activation, approved submission rate, refund rate, support contact rate, failed payment rate, search zero-result rate, accessibility defects, and service error rate.

## 9. Risks
Cold-start inventory; quality inconsistency; intellectual-property complaints; malicious uploads; Microsoft trademark confusion; licensing ambiguity; payment/tax complexity; creator support burden; search spam; dependency on platform changes; overbuilding before demand validation.

## 10. Assumptions requiring validation
Users will exchange registration for valuable free assets; technical metadata improves purchase confidence; enterprise teams value governed bundles; creators will accept moderation; SEO can acquire high-intent traffic; manual creator payouts are acceptable before multi-vendor scale.
