# Competitor review: Lumeric Visuals (Power BI)

Reviewed 2026-09-30 from the public site (https://lumericvisuals.com/), at the product owner's request. This is a summary in our own words. Nothing was copied (see `docs/final-decisions.md`, "Launch content plan approved; competitor research rule").

## What they offer
- **Products:** custom Power BI *visuals* (`.pbiviz` add-ons), not templates or components. There is one released product, a Calendar Heatmap, and more are on a roadmap.
- **Presentation:** a live, interactive demo on the home page, running the visual on several sample datasets. The product proves itself; there are no static screenshots.
- **Customer:** organisations, with an emphasis on deploying the visual across a whole organisation.
- **Support:** email only. There are no public docs, forum or learning content.
- **Commercial:** no prices are published. AppSource listing is pending, and no Microsoft partnership is claimed.

## Gaps LowCodeStacks can fill
| Their gap | Our answer |
|---|---|
| One product | A growing catalogue across six technology sections |
| No prices shown | Prices shown on every paid item; most items free |
| No learning content | A tutorial and KPI guide next to each product |
| Email-only support | Discussion under items (approach to be decided by the product owner) |
| No evidence shown | Licence, version and compatibility evidence on every product page (built, MVP-005) |

## Ideas to adopt (as our own implementation)
- **Live previews:** each KPI or component page shows the item working on sample data, not only a screenshot.
- Keep product pages focused on one thing, with a clear demo first.

## Possible future product type
Custom Power BI visuals are a distinct category, with their own packaging and AppSource certification. Out of scope today; noted for later.
