# SEO audit and plan (2026-10-06)

**Status:** research and proposal. Nothing here is approved until it's recorded in `docs/final-decisions.md`.

## What the live site serves today
Checked on lowcodestacks.com, 2026-10-06:
- `robots.txt`: `Allow: /`, `Disallow: /api/`, and an absolute `Sitemap:` line. Correct.
- `sitemap.xml`: valid, but only **4 URLs**: home, about, privacy and terms. **No guides**, because none is PUBLISHED yet. Articles join the sitemap automatically, with an accurate `lastmod`, once they're published.
- Home page: `index, follow`, a self-canonical link, and a title. `www` redirects to the apex domain (301).

## Already built (code)
- **Robots:** deny by default, with per-page opt-in to `index`. `X-Robots-Tag: noindex` on API, account and sign-in responses.
- **Canonicals:** canonical URLs and a canonical policy for query variants (MVP-021).
- **Structured data:**
  - article pages have `TechArticle` JSON-LD with `datePublished`, `dateModified`, and the brand Organization as author and publisher;
  - `BreadcrumbList`;
  - `WebSite` on the home page.
- **Sitemap:** includes published articles, the `/learn` hub and technology sections that have content, with real `lastmod`.
- **Share images:** generated share images (`/og/learn/{slug}`).
- **Article pages:** breadcrumbs, table of contents, reading time, last-updated date, related guides and an RSS feed for updates.

## Gaps, against Google Search Central
| # | Gap | Evidence | Proposed fix | Owner |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Nothing to crawl yet | The live sitemap has 4 URLs | Publish the drafts in `/admin/content` | PO |
| 2 | Google doesn't know the site | No Search Console property | Verify the domain with a DNS TXT record at Porkbun, then submit `sitemap.xml` in the Sitemaps report | PO |
| 3 | Bing and other engines | No Bing Webmaster Tools; no IndexNow | Bing Webmaster Tools can import the Search Console property. Optionally, ping **IndexNow** when an article is published. Bing and others take part; Google isn't mentioned as a participant | PO, then agent (needs approval: sends page URLs to api.indexnow.org) |
| 4 | Article JSON-LD has no `image` | Google recommends images (16:9, 4:3 or 1:1, at least 50K pixels) | Add the existing 1200×630 share image as `image` | Agent |
| 5 | Readers can't see *who* wrote a page, *how*, and *why* | Google's guidance on generative AI: fact-check before publishing, and consider explaining how the content was created | A public **"How we write and check guides"** page (research → Microsoft Learn check → human review → dates), linked from every guide. Visible "checked on" dates (MVP-038) | Agent (wording needs PO approval) |
| 6 | Hub pages are thin until filled | — | MVP-037 hub framing, and 2–4 items per section | Agent |
| 7 | Page speed must survive the new visuals and motion | Core Web Vitals: LCP ≤ 2.5 s, INP < 200 ms, CLS < 0.1 | Set width and height on every image, lazy-load below the fold, use CSS-only motion that rests on the final frame, and add a Lighthouse budget check | Agent |
| 8 | Structured data that no longer helps | HowTo rich results deprecated (2023); FAQ rich results removed from Google Search in May 2026 | **Don't** add HowTo or FAQ markup. Keep TechArticle and BreadcrumbList | — |
| 9 | Fields Google ignores | Google ignores `priority` and `changefreq` in sitemaps; it uses `lastmod` only when it's accurate | Keep the current sitemap (no priority or changefreq, real `lastmod`) | — |
| 10 | URL stability | Changing URLs after indexing costs ranking signals | Freeze `/learn/{slug}` before the first publish. Slugs never change; if one must, add a 301 | Agent |
