## Explicit non-conversions required by this research's own instruction

None of the following conversions were made anywhere in this research package,
and none should be made by anyone extending it later:

- **Subscribers were not converted into users.** No subscriber count was even
  found for either competitor, so this risk didn't arise in practice, but the
  rule is stated for future research passes that might find one.
- **Employer logos were not converted into corporate procurement.** PowerLibs's
  displayed logos (Microsoft, Porsche Consulting, Stellantis, McLaren Group,
  Deutsche Bahn, Safran, Röchling, McCormick, Stadler Rail, National Grid,
  CarMax, EDEKA) are recorded exactly as what they are — a vendor's own
  marketing display — and nowhere in this package is the presence of a logo
  treated as evidence that the named organization purchased, licensed, or
  endorsed PowerLibs at an organizational level. The most plausible
  explanation for any single logo is that one individual employee at that
  organization is a personal subscriber.
- **Video views were not converted into product purchases.** No video-view
  data was found or used.
- **Repository stars were not converted into active customers.** Where star
  counts appear in this package (Microsoft's own repositories in
  `01-market-landscape.md` and `02-competitor-evidence.md`), they are
  presented as GitHub activity/interest signals only, never as a customer or
  revenue proxy.
- **First-party user counts were not converted into independently verified
  adoption**, because none were found to convert in the first place — this is
  recorded as a genuine gap (see `11-open-research-questions.md`), not papered
  over with an inferred number.
