`10-current-architecture-inventory.md` for the repeated confirmation of

## Reading this matrix

- **`NotRecommendedFromEvidence` appears twice** — both for advertising
  activation specifically, both because the evidence (traffic thresholds,
  no CSP/feature-flag infrastructure yet) doesn't support it *right now*,
  not because advertising is rejected as a model forever.
- **`BlockedByDecision` appears three times** — checkout (blocked by
  unresolved pricing/tax decisions), commission calculation (blocked by the
  unresolved rate), and automated payouts (blocked by an explicit,
  deliberate deferral this research does not reverse).
- **No row is `Approved`** — that value does not exist in the allowed set,
  deliberately.
- **The single most-repeated `RequiredChangeType` combination** across paid-
  asset-adjacent rows is `Schema + Domain Logic` — consistent with
  `10-current-architecture-inventory.md`'s finding that Commerce and Creator
  are genuinely unbuilt domains, not partially-built ones.
