---
title: "Power Automate mobile app retired"
slug: power-automate-mobile-app-retired
kind: RETIREMENT
technology: POWER_AUTOMATE
action: "Move approvers to Teams"
source: https://learn.microsoft.com/power-platform/important-changes-coming#deprecation-of-the-power-automate-mobile-app
effective: 2026-08-31
replacement: "Approvals app in Microsoft Teams"
---
From August 31, 2026, Microsoft no longer supports the iOS and Android app and removes it from the app stores. Cloud flows and approvals keep working. Approvers should use the Approvals app in Teams, and flows that send mobile notifications should switch to a Teams message or an email.
