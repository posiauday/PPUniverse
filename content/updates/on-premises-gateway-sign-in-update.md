title: "Older on-premises data gateways need an update to keep signing in"

kind: RETIREMENT
technology: POWER_BI
action: "Update gateways to May 2026+"
source: https://learn.microsoft.com/data-integration/gateway/service-gateway-update-signin
effective: 2026-08-31
replacement: "May 2026 or later gateway release"
---
Microsoft changed how gateways sign in, and finished applying the change to every tenant by 31 August 2026. Gateways older than the May 2026 release, or January–April 2026 releases below the patched build, may fail to sign in when you install, configure, recover or manage them. Update them, one cluster member at a time.
