# Product Discovery

Use when a feature request is broad, ambiguous, or not traced to an approved requirement. Read BRD, PRD, open questions and backlog. Produce problem statement, persona, journey, scope, business rule, acceptance criteria, metrics, risks, dependencies and decision owner. Do not code. Add unresolved decisions to open questions.

## Output
Provide a concise decision, evidence reviewed, requirement IDs, findings, changes made or required, tests, risks and follow-up owner.
