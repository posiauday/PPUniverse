# Seo Content

Use for landing pages, products, tutorials and collections. Define search intent, unique value, outline, internal links, author/reviewer evidence, version/prerequisites, limitations, metadata, canonical and structured data eligibility. Never scrape or imitate competitor text.

## Output
Provide a concise decision, evidence reviewed, requirement IDs, findings, changes made or required, tests, risks and follow-up owner.
